Run the Assessment

This assessment runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are never transmitted, never stored, and are discarded when you leave the page. There is no account, no email gate, and no tracking.

1 · Intent Alignment

The primary pages receiving search traffic were designed around a defined search intent, documented before the page was written.

On each major entry page, the first screen addresses the question the visitor searched before introducing anything else.

2 · Visibility Conditions

Every indexable page has a unique title, a controlled meta description, exactly one H1, and a canonical URL.

The asset's key pages are indexed and appear for the queries they were designed to serve.

3 · Experience Quality

Key pages load quickly and are fully usable on mobile devices without layout breakage.

Core content remains readable and navigable when JavaScript fails or loads slowly.

4 · Trust Signal Coherence

Claims on commercial pages are supported by evidence; no guarantees or performance promises the asset cannot substantiate.

Identity, terminology, and visual presentation are consistent across every page of the asset.

5 · Navigation Clarity

From any entry page, the intended next step is visible and evident without hunting or guesswork.

No page is a dead end: every page links onward to a purposeful next destination.

6 · Action Pathway Integrity

Each key page has one clearly defined terminal action, and it completes without friction on all devices.

Nothing interrupts the path to action: no popups, forced steps, or competing calls to action.

7 · Strategic Outcome Alignment

The actions the asset drives are the ones the operation actually needs, and they are measured.

Those measurements are reviewed on a defined cadence, and findings feed back into the asset.
